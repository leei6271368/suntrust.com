GET TO KNOW US

Company Profile

Suntrust Properties, Inc. (SPI) is a 100% wholly-owned subsidiary of Megaworld Corporation, a company under the umbrella of the Alliance Global Group, Inc.

In 1997, during the economic crises that affected Asia and other rising markets, SPI was conceived and has since remained an industry player, known for aggressively expanding its portfolio through the continuous development of vertical and horizontal residential communities across Luzon, Visayas, and Mindanao.

Through its acquisition of 42-year-old developer Stateland, Inc. (SLI) in 2018, Suntrust Properties envisions a bigger, better, and brighter future.

With a combined real estate development track record of 65 years, SPI and SLI continue to grow and expand their reach to provide quality homes to Filipino families.
