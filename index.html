ABOUT SUNTRUST

More than houses.
A place for life.

Suntrust Properties, Inc. develops residential communities designed to help people find a place to call home. Our public website introduces our company, properties, operations, sustainability efforts, team, and career opportunities.

We believe a home is more than a building. It is where families grow, memories are made, and communities take shape.

Our Mission

To create thoughtfully planned residential communities and provide a positive homebuying experience.

Our Vision

To be a trusted choice for homebuyers seeking welcoming communities and lasting value.
