GET TO KNOW US

More than houses.
A place for life.

Suntrust Properties, Inc. (SPI) was established in 1997 and develops residential communities for Filipino families. Its portfolio includes horizontal and vertical developments across Luzon, Visayas, and Mindanao.

Suntrust is a wholly owned subsidiary of Megaworld Corporation, part of Alliance Global Group, Inc. In 2018, Suntrust acquired Stateland, Inc., extending its experience in residential development.
