WHO WE ARE

About Suntrust Properties, Inc.

Suntrust Properties, Inc. (SPI) is a Philippine real estate developer established in 1997. The company develops residential communities, including horizontal and vertical developments, serving homebuyers across Luzon, Visayas, and Mindanao.

Suntrust is a wholly owned subsidiary of Megaworld Corporation, which is part of Alliance Global Group, Inc. In 2018, Suntrust acquired Stateland, Inc., expanding its real estate development experience.
